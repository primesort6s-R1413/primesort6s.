WHAT 6S CHANGES

THE SYSTEM

Six pillars, run in order.

Each stage builds the discipline the next one depends on — skip one and the standard doesn't hold.

1

Sort

Separate what's needed from what isn't. Red-tag anything unused for 30 days and clear it from the floor.

2

Set in Order

Give every remaining item a marked, obvious home — shadow boards, floor tape, labeled bins.

3

Shine

Clean as inspection. A wipe-down surfaces leaks, wear, and damage before they become downtime.

4

Standardize

Turn the first three into a written, visual standard anyone on the team can check against.

5

Sustain

Build the audit rhythm and ownership that keeps the standard alive after the consultants leave.

6

Safety

Close the loop: hazard lines, PPE zones, and clear sightlines built into the layout, not bolted on.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

Four ways in, one standard out.

01 — AUDIT

Floor assessment

A walk-the-line audit scoring every bay against the 6S checklist, with photos, red-tag counts, and a priority map of where to start.

02 — IMPLEMENTATION

Physical rollout

Shadow boards, floor tape, labeling, and layout changes installed on-site with your team, not around them.

03 — TRAINING

Team certification

Shift-by-shift training so every operator can run and defend an audit, not just follow one.

04 — SUSTAINMENT

Ongoing audits

Monthly scoring and a scorecard dashboard so drift gets caught in weeks, not at the next big cleanup.

PROVEN ON THE FLOOR

What a completed 6S rollout looks like.

From a recent industrial site engagement — a process manufacturing facility in Bangalore. Client details withheld; the practices are what matter.

TABLE & WORKSTATION MARKING

Every item, a taped outline

Monitors, keyboards, and shared tools got fixed, labeled positions on desks and control panels — so a missing item is visible in seconds, not discovered at the next shift.

FLOOR & FLOW MARKING

Lines that tell you where to walk and where things flow

Pedestrian paths, equipment footprints, and pipe flow-direction arrows were painted directly onto the floor and machinery, cutting near-miss incidents around moving vehicles and hot surfaces.

TOOLS & SPARES ARRANGEMENT

Shadow boards down to the wrench size

Tool boards and spares cupboards were re-organized with labeled, shadow-outlined slots for every item — inside and outside each cupboard — so anyone can spot what's missing at a glance.

EQUIPMENT & PANEL NAMING

Every panel, switch, and feeder labeled

MCC panels, switches, sockets, and machine names were tagged consistently across the site, removing the guesswork during changeovers, faults, and lockout-tagout procedures.

THIRD-PARTY AUDITED 6S & VISUAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

This engagement's 6S and Visual Management System implementation was independently audited and certified against ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 quality and safety-management standards — verifying the rollout met all 6S requirements on site.
